## Retrieval

`recall@k`: a chunk containing the labelled evidence is in the top k. `paper_hit@5`: any chunk from the right paper is in the top 5. `mrr@10`: mean reciprocal rank of the first evidence chunk. `raw` embeds the user's text as typed, `rewritten` embeds the LLM's RETRIEVE line, `paper_scoped` is the production path (rewritten query + paper-filtered search merged with general search). The untrained-MLP rows reproduce the pre-fix embedding (averaged over random initialisations, with the Recall@5 range across seeds).
